Giulio Franchina

Fashion Designer | Product-Driven Design, Brand Identity & Collection Development

Fashion Designer with a strong product-driven mindset and a design approach centered on the garment itself. My approach to design is rooted in a solid understanding of the product, developed through a long-standing, early-developed interest and continuous personal research, later structured and refined through formal fashion design education. My engagement with fashion spans its entire spectrum: from historical and iconic garments, to archival pieces, workwear, high fashion, and streetwear. I focus on product authenticity, construction, and identity, working on collections as coherent systems rather than isolated pieces. A central aspect of my practice is archive-based research and the study of garments and brands that endure over time, where identity is built through consistency, proportion, and cultural depth rather than short-term trends. I am particularly strong in: – product and garment design – archive and typology research – collection development and coherence – brand identity construction, from research to product translation Brand identity, for me, is not a stylistic layer, but the result of a rigorous design process involving research, materials, construction, and form. The product itself is the primary carrier of meaning. Alongside design, I use tools such as Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Unreal Engine as supportive instruments for research, visualization, and experimentation within the fashion and product design process. I value collaboration and clear communication, and I work effectively within teams with a rational, precise, and grounded approach. My goal is to grow within environments that respect product culture, design integrity, and thoughtful innovation.
